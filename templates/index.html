Scoring Reference
4% – 41% Likely Authentic Media

Noise floor, frequency decay, and/or camera EXIF resemble real camera capture. Below 35% is strongly authentic.

42% – 67% Uncertain / Mixed Signals

Inconclusive range—often caused by heavy JPEG compression, screenshots, filters, or stripped metadata. Do not treat as proof.

68% – 98% Likely AI-Generated

Multiple synthetic anomalies align. 75%+ indicates strong AI evidence; 92%+ appears when explicit AI metadata tags are embedded.
